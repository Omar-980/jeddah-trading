Privacy Policy

For the Jeddah Trading shop and app · shopjeddahtrading.com · last updated 23 October 2026

This explains what Jeddah Trading keeps about you, why we keep it, who can see it, and how to have it removed. We have tried to write it in plain language rather than legal language.

What we collect, and when

When you…We store
Place an orderYour name, phone number, and — for home delivery — your address and area. For orders to the United Kingdom, your email address and postcode. The payment method you chose, and the payment screenshot if you upload one.
Create an accountThe same details, plus your 4-digit PIN. The PIN is stored only as a one-way scramble — nobody at Jeddah Trading can read it or tell you what it is.
Message us in the shopYour name, phone number and the messages you send, so we can answer you.
Shop while signed inWhat you bought, your loyalty points, your referral code, your saved items, and your basket — so it is still there on your other phone.
Write a reviewThe name and town you type on the review. These are shown publicly on the product.
Just browseYour basket, your saved items and recently viewed products are kept on your own phone and are not sent to us unless you are signed in.

Why we keep it

  • To take your order, deliver it, and answer you if something goes wrong.
  • To keep our own accounts and stock records, which we are required to keep.
  • To run the loyalty points and referral codes you take part in.

Who can see it

  • Our staff, according to what each person's role allows.
  • The person delivering your order, who is given your name, phone and address so they can find you.
  • Our hosting provider, which runs the servers the shop sits on.
  • If you choose to send your order on WhatsApp, that message goes through WhatsApp.

We do not sell your details to anyone, and we do not share them with advertisers. When we are running adverts, our website may load Meta's (Facebook) measurement tool, which tells Meta that a page was viewed or a purchase happened. It is not given your name, your phone number or your address.

How long we keep it

Your account details are kept until you delete your account. Order records are kept as part of our business accounts; when you delete your account the personal details on past orders are erased, and only the order number, date, items and totals remain, with no name attached.

Your choices

  • See or correct your details — open My Account in the shop or the app.
  • Delete your account and your personal details — see below. You do not need the app installed.
  • Stop the emails — if you signed up for offers, tell us and we will remove you.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and your personal details at any time, either from My Account inside the shop or the app, or from the page below, which works without installing anything.

What is erased: your name, phone number, email, address, PIN, loyalty points, referral code, saved items and your whole message history with us.
What remains: the order number, date, items and totals of past orders, with your name and contact details removed. We keep these because they are part of our accounts.

Children

The shop is meant for adults. We do not knowingly create accounts for children. If you believe a child has given us their details, tell us and we will remove them.

Security

The shop is served over an encrypted connection. PINs are stored scrambled and cannot be read back. Staff access is limited by role, and every change to an order or to stock is recorded against the person who made it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your details we will update this page and change the date at the top.
